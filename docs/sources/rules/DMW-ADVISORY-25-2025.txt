TRANSCRIPTION (key passages, verbatim) of DMW-ADVISORY-25-2025.pdf (scanned; no text layer).
Department of Migrant Workers, ADVISORY NO. 25, Series of 2025. Signed: Hans Leo J. Cacdac, Secretary. 22 August 2025.
Title: Adoption of the Enhanced Reform Programs for Filipino Domestic Workers and Promotion of their Rights, Dignity, and Welfare

[1. Increase in Minimum Monthly Wage of Domestic Workers] (page 1)
"To ensure just remuneration and uphold the right of every domestic worker to fair wages, the Department shall
implement an increase in the minimum monthly wage for Filipino domestic workers from USD 400 to at least USD 500,
without prejudice to existing rules and policies implementing a higher wage and subject to the following:
• The increase shall be integrated into all employment contracts processed by the Department of Migrant Workers (DMW);
• The new wage floor reflects the recognition of domestic work as work of equal value deserving of fair compensation; and
• Migrant Workers Offices (MWOs) are directed to engage host governments and foreign recruitment agencies to ensure
  the discussion and implementation of this wage standard."

[2. Annual Medical Check-ups and Hospitalization/Treatment] (page 1)
"In cases of work-related accidents and illnesses, the PRAs and principals (FRAs and foreign employers) shall cause
the hospitalization and/or treatment of OFW domestic workers."

[3. Implementation of Mandatory "Know Your Employer" (KYE) Protocol] (pages 1-2)
"• Mandatory video conferencing between the prospective domestic worker and employer shall be conducted before
  contract finalization;
• Allow the worker gain direct knowledge of job duties, workplace conditions, household composition, rest periods,
  and accommodation;
• Obtain full, prior, and informed consent of the worker before deployment; ..."

[7. Whitelisting Policy for Recruitment Agencies] (page 3)
"...the Department issued Memorandum Circular No. 04, Series of 2024, establishing a whitelisting system for PRAs and
FRAs for countries with whitelisting requirements. Only agencies with proven compliance and ethical practices may
deploy workers, and each must designate a welfare desk officer."

[8. Rights-based Approach ... AKSYON Fund] (page 3)
"The Department reiterates that passports are the property of the Philippine Government and must remain with the
worker at all times, pursuant to Section 13 of R.A. 8239 (Philippine Passport Act of 1996) as amended. Employers,
recruiters, and third parties are prohibited from withholding passports except for justified purposes such as visa
stamping, after which they must be promptly returned. Violations shall result in disciplinary action, including
suspension, cancellation, or disqualification."
(pages 3-4) "The Department also maintains the strict implementation of the Standard Employment Contract (SEC) for
domestic workers to guarantee decent accommodation, adequate living conditions, and at least one full rest day per
week, complemented by a proposed annual mandatory medical check-up in coordination with host governments and
overseas posts."

[COMPLIANCE AND ENFORCEMENT] (page 4)
"Reforms under item numbers 1-4 of this Advisory shall apply to all PRAs and principals (FRAs and foreign employers)
with newly hired domestic workers under employment contracts entered into, or with vacationing or returning domestic
workers under contracts renewed, 60 days after issuance thereof."
