## Part 1: Official red flags (verbatim, cited)

| # | Red flag | Exact wording | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Recruiter works only through social media / chat apps | "Avoid transacting with persons operating only through Facebook, Tiktok, Telegram, WhatsApp, or other unofficial channels." | DMW, via GMA News, 4 May 2026: https://www.gmanetwork.com/news/topstories/nation/986364/dmw-illegal-recruiters-middle-east/story/ |
| R2 | Fees before a contract | "Never pay placement fees without a valid employment contract and official receipt." | same |
| R3 | Tourist visa for work | "Do not accept arrangements involving tourist visas for employment purposes." | same |
| R3b | | "It's important that your visa is not a tourist visa. It's important that it's an employment visa." (Usec. Bernard Olalia) | Daily Tribune, 3 Jun 2025: https://tribune.net.ph/amp/story/2025/06/03/fake-job-offers-flood-social-media-dmw-urges-ofw-hopefuls-to-stay-cautious |
| R4 | Unlicensed agency | "Verify whether the recruitment agency is duly licensed by the DMW" | GMA, 4 May 2026 |
| R5 | Unapproved job order | "Confirm that the job order is approved and valid" | GMA, 4 May 2026 |
| R6 | Fixers / travel agencies | "Do not transact with travel agencies, fixers, or unauthorized representatives offering overseas jobs" | GMA, 4 May 2026 |
| R7 | Payment to a GCash number | "they'll send a GCash number where you're expected to send payment." (Olalia; such transactions are "red flags") | Daily Tribune, 3 Jun 2025 |
| R8 | Clone pages of real agencies | "Around 80,000 of these clones have already been taken down across various platforms." (Olalia) | Daily Tribune, 3 Jun 2025 |
